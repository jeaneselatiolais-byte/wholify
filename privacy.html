Privacy Policy

Effective April 2026 · Publisher: Jeanese Latiolais (appsbyai) · Contact: jeanese.latiolais@gmail.com

Quick summary

  • No account. No sign-up. No email collection.
  • No ads. No analytics SDKs. No advertising identifier.
  • Your dietary profile, saved recipes, saved swaps, and conversion history live on your device.
  • Ingredient swaps are sent to our server, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API. Each request carries a random per-device UUID (Keychain-stored) for rate limiting — it's not linked to your Apple ID or any personal identity.

1. What stays on your device

These data live in the on-device database (SwiftData) and never sync to our servers:

  • Dietary profile (dietary goals, allergens, disclaimer acknowledgement)
  • Saved swaps, saved recipes, favorite conversions
  • Conversion history and recent swap queries
  • Local cache of previous swap responses (for offline fallback)

Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

2. What goes to our server

When you use the Swap feature or paste a recipe URL/text, we send the following over HTTPS to our Cloudflare Worker proxy, which forwards the ingredient-substitution portion to Anthropic's Claude API:

  • The ingredient name or recipe URL/text you submitted
  • Your active dietary goals (e.g., "lower sugar", "higher fiber")
  • Your active allergens (e.g., "dairy-free", "peanut-free")
  • A random per-device UUID (the X-Wholify-Device-Id header), used only to apply free-tier daily rate limits and to assign an A/B experimental variant. This UUID:
    • Is stored in your device Keychain (not iCloud-synced)
    • Is not linked to your Apple ID, email, name, or any personal identifier
    • Cannot be used to identify you across other apps, websites, or advertisers
    • Cannot be recovered by us — if you reinstall the app, a new UUID is generated

Requests are logged in Cloudflare's standard edge logs (short retention, IP-level) for abuse prevention. We do not associate request content with your UUID for long-term storage.

3. What we don't collect

  • No account, email, name, phone number, address
  • No location data of any kind
  • No contacts, calendar, reminders, photos
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) — ATT is not used because we don't track
  • No analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Segment, no in-house analytics beyond the paywall funnel telemetry described in §5)
  • No crash-reporting SDKs that collect device fingerprints

4. Voice input

Voice transcription uses Apple's on-device Speech framework (SFSpeechRecognizer with requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true). Audio bytes never leave your device. If on-device recognition is unavailable for your locale, the feature fails fast rather than falling back to server-side recognition.

5. Paywall funnel telemetry

When the paywall is shown, dismissed, or a purchase/restore succeeds, we send a small event to our server describing the event type and the reason the paywall was shown (e.g., "daily healthy swap limit reached"). These events include only:

  • The event type (impression, dismissed, purchased, restored)
  • The reason the paywall was shown (a fixed enum — e.g., "dietary_profile_cap")
  • Your A/B variant assignment (e.g., "A")
  • The app version (e.g., "1.0.0 (42)")
  • The X-Wholify-Device-Id described in §2

No ingredient text, recipe content, or personal information is included in these events.

6. Third parties

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes ingredient-substitution and recipe-parsing requests. Anthropic's own policies govern how they handle the request content on their end; they do not receive your device UUID, Apple ID, or any identifier we don't have ourselves.
  • Cloudflare Workers — hosts our API proxy. Cloudflare's standard edge log retention applies to request metadata (IPs, timestamps, path).
  • Apple (StoreKit) — handles all subscription and tip-jar purchases. We never see payment-card, bank, or billing information; Apple provides us only an opaque purchase-verification receipt.

No other third parties receive any data from Wholify.

7. Data retention

  • On-device data — kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Use Settings → Delete saved data to wipe recipes, swaps, conversions, and caches (your dietary profile is preserved; uninstall the app to remove that).
  • Server logs — request metadata (path, timestamps, device UUID, IP) is retained by Cloudflare per their default log retention (typically 30 days for Workers logs). Swap-response cache entries live up to 24 hours keyed by a normalized ingredient string.
  • Paywall telemetry — aggregated for A/B analysis. Individual events are retained up to 90 days.

8. Children

Wholify is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The app is rated 4+ because it is suitable for all ages, not because it is targeted at children.

9. Your controls

  • Delete saved data — Settings → Data → Delete saved data removes everything stored on-device except your dietary profile.
  • Uninstall the app — removes the dietary profile and the per-device UUID.
  • Disable voice / camera permissions — iOS Settings → Wholify → revoke at any time.
  • Cancel subscription — iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Request data deletion — since no account-tied data is retained server-side, uninstalling the app accomplishes this. Contact us at the email above if you want us to verify no lingering request logs reference your device UUID.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page and the in-app Privacy sheet if anything material changes. If we ever add an analytics SDK, ad SDK, or user-account system, that will be announced in-app before the change takes effect.
