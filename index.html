Three tools, one focused app

Offline unit converter

Volume, weight, temperature, plus ingredient-aware volume-to-weight with 30+ preloaded densities. Works with no network.

AI ingredient swaps

Standard substitutes when you're out of something; Healthy alternatives tuned to your dietary goals. Ratio-aware, not a lookup table.

Recipe scaling

Manually enter or paste a recipe URL, scale up or down, tap any measurement to convert or any ingredient to swap.

Allergen safety first

Set allergens once. Every AI response is filtered server-side before it reaches you — even hidden sources like casein or semolina. Never gated behind Pro.

Privacy you can actually read

No account. No sign-up. No email collection. No ads. No analytics SDKs. No advertising identifier.

Your dietary profile, saved recipes, and conversion history live on your device. Ingredient swaps travel through our proxy to Anthropic's Claude API with a random per-device UUID that exists only for rate limiting — it's not linked to your Apple ID or any personal identity.

Voice input runs on-device via Apple's Speech framework. Audio never leaves your phone.
